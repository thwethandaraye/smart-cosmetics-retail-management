# Company Background

> **Note:** Terra Tint Cosmetics is a fictional/simulated company
> created for this Business Analysis case study. The business
> processes and operational context described in this document are
> assumptions developed for the purpose of analyzing and designing
> a proposed retail management system.

## 1. Company Overview

Terra Tint Cosmetics is a simulated cosmetics retailer that sells a
range of beauty and personal-care products to customers across
multiple international markets. The company's product portfolio
includes skincare, haircare, and other cosmetic products.

The business processes customer orders, manages product information,
coordinates sales activities, fulfills orders, and monitors sales
performance. As the business grows, effective management of
operational information becomes increasingly important for supporting
daily activities and business decision-making.

## 2. Business Model

Terra Tint Cosmetics operates as a retail business in which customers
purchase cosmetic products through digital sales channels. The
business receives customer orders, confirms product availability,
processes payments, prepares orders for fulfillment, and coordinates
delivery to customers.

Sales personnel support customer purchases and sales activities,
while management is responsible for monitoring business performance
and making operational decisions.

## 3. Products and Services

The company offers a variety of cosmetics and personal-care products.
The sales dataset used in this case study contains 15 different
products.

Examples of product types represented in the dataset include:

- Facial moisturizers
- Facial serums
- Cleansers
- Toners
- Hair-care products
- Body-care products

The product portfolio is used as a basis for analyzing differences
in product sales performance and identifying opportunities for
improved product monitoring.

## 4. Geographic Market

Terra Tint Cosmetics serves customers across multiple countries.
The dataset contains sales transactions associated with six countries:

- United States
- New Zealand
- Australia
- United Kingdom
- Canada
- India

The geographic distribution of sales provides an opportunity to
compare regional performance and support data-driven business
decision-making.

## 5. Sales Operations

Sales activities are supported by sales personnel who are responsible
for processing customer purchases and recording sales transactions.

The dataset contains transactions associated with 10 salespeople.
Sales performance can therefore be analyzed by salesperson to
identify differences in sales contribution and transaction activity.

The proposed system will provide centralized sales information to
support sales monitoring and performance analysis.

## 6. Business Operations

The simulated business involves several interconnected operational
activities:

1. Customer interaction and product selection
2. Order recording
3. Product availability checking
4. Payment confirmation
5. Order fulfillment
6. Delivery
7. Sales transaction recording
8. Management reporting and analysis

These activities require information to move between different
business functions. Effective information management is therefore
important for maintaining operational visibility and supporting
business decisions.

## 7. Current Technology Environment

For the purpose of this case study, the current business environment
is assumed to rely on a combination of digital sales channels,
transaction records, spreadsheets, and separate reporting activities.

This environment may become increasingly difficult to manage as
transaction volume and operational complexity increase. A centralized
system is therefore proposed to integrate key business information
and reduce fragmentation between operational activities.

> **Assumption:** The current technology environment is simulated for
> this case study and should not be interpreted as verified
> information about an actual company.

## 8. Business Growth Context

As the number of products, customers, transactions, and sales
activities increases, the business requires better visibility into
its operations.

The sales dataset demonstrates differences in performance across
countries, products, salespeople, and months. These variations create
a need for structured reporting and analytics that can help
management monitor business performance and identify trends.

Future business growth may also increase the need for centralized
customer, order, inventory, product, and user information.

## 9. Business Analysis Context

This case study examines how a centralized technology solution could
support the operational and analytical needs of Terra Tint Cosmetics.

The analysis begins with the available sales data and uses the
identified business patterns as evidence for defining business
problems and opportunities. These findings will then be used to
analyze stakeholders, document the current AS-IS process, identify
pain points and gaps, and define requirements for the proposed
Smart Cosmetics Retail Management System.

## 10. Summary

Terra Tint Cosmetics is a simulated international cosmetics retailer
with multiple products, salespeople, and geographic markets. The
business requires effective management of sales and operational
information to support day-to-day activities and informed decision-
making.

The proposed project will investigate how a centralized retail
management system can improve information visibility, sales
monitoring, reporting, and operational coordination while providing
a scalable foundation for future business needs.
